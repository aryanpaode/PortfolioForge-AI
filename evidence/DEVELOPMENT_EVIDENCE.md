## How the outputs changed

* **24 August — early prototype:** An Android emulator could display a resume in the system print preview.
* **26 September — presentation refinement:** A subsequent emulator print preview showed the resume with the red-and-white styling, section dividers, and a populated profile/skills section.
* **Initial portfolio export:** HTML generation worked, but most uploaded-document content was not reflected in the generated sections.
* **Later output:** After document-specific extraction, verification and profile-data improvements, additional sections appeared in the portfolio and PDF resume.
